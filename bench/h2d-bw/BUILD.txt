Build and run the H2D/D2H bandwidth probe.

  g++ -O2 -std=c++17 -I D:/Scoop/apps/vulkan/current/Include h2d-bw.cpp \
      -o h2d-bw.exe -L D:/Scoop/apps/vulkan/current/Lib -l vulkan-1

  ./h2d-bw.exe                 # 512 MiB per test (default)
  ./h2d-bw.exe 1073741824      # 1 GiB per test

Raw Vulkan, no ggml/llama dependency. Picks the discrete GPU, allocates host-visible
(WC and cached, persistently mapped and map-then-free) plus device-local buffers, then
times vkCmdCopyBuffer legs under: chunk size, submit pipelining depth, queue family,
mapping mode, concurrent legs, host-RAM saturation, and a pageable->pinned->device
staging pipeline. Rates are decimal GB/s (1e9 B/s) unless a row says GiB/s.
